The 45-Minute Reading Data Chat

A protocol for the meeting after each benchmark window, so screener data turns into groups and next steps the same week.

Before the meeting

  • Pull screener results by class, with the composite and every subtest.
  • Flag students who moved up or down a tier since the last window.
  • Bring diagnostic results (a phonics survey, for example) for students below benchmark.
  • Share one sheet per class so the team looks at the same thing.

Ground rules

  • Talk about skills, not labels. "Blending is behind," not "she's low."
  • Every concern ends with an action and a date.
  • Growth counts. A student far below benchmark rarely closes the gap in one window.

The agenda

TimeStepGuiding questions
5 minCelebrateWho moved up a tier? What were we doing for those students?
10 minFind the patternWhich subskill is lowest across the grade? If many students share it, it is a whole-class (Tier 1) issue. If a few do, it is a group issue.
15 minStudents at riskFor each student: which subskill is behind? Has it grown since the last window? Is growth steady, slowing or stalled? Who is on the cusp and could move with a small push?
10 minRegroupMatch each group to one skill gap (see the table on page 2). Who teaches each group, when, and for how long?
5 minCommitWho does what, starting when? When do we check progress next? Which families need a call?

Read the subtests, not just the composite

Two students with the same composite can need very different help. A student who reads nonsense words sound by sound but rarely blends them into whole words needs blending practice, not more letter-sound review. On Acadience Nonsense Word Fluency, that shows up as a solid Correct Letter Sounds score with a low Whole Words Read score.

Match the need to the support

Is it enough progress?

  • Is the student moving toward benchmark?
  • Are skills developing in order, or are there big gaps?
  • After 6 to 8 weeks of consistent, well-matched support with little growth, change something: time, group size, intensity or approach.
  • If that change doesn't help either, bring the student to your intervention team (I&RS or MTSS) with the progress data.

A common rhythm

  • Benchmark: beginning, middle and end of year for every student.
  • Tier 2: progress monitoring every 2 to 3 weeks.
  • Tier 3: progress monitoring every 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Team check-in: every 4 to 6 weeks between windows.
